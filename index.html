Iconic Sri Lanka Destinations

Sigiriya Rock Fortress

The 5th-century royal sky citadel of King Kashyapa. A UNESCO World Heritage Site rising 200 metres above the jungle canopy of the Cultural Triangle. Ancient frescoes, mirror-wall inscriptions, and the iconic Lion Gate await at the summit.

Ella & Nine Arch Bridge

Mist-clad tea highlands of the central Sri Lanka hill country. The iconic Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam's Peak, and Ella Rock offer dramatic viewpoints over sweeping valleys. Home to award-winning single-estate Ceylon teas.

Yala National Park — Big 5 Safari

The world's highest density of wild leopards. Sri Lanka's premier wildlife sanctuary encompasses over 979 km² of dry-zone scrub jungle, lagoons, and coastal beaches. Home to elephants, sloth bears, crocodiles, and over 215 bird species.

Galle Fort — Dutch Colonial Heritage

A UNESCO-listed 17th-century Dutch colonial fortification on Sri Lanka's southern coast. Cobblestone streets, pastel colonial architecture, boutique galleries, and panoramic Indian Ocean views from the rampart walls.

Mirissa — Whale Watching & Surf

Sri Lanka's premier whale-watching destination. Blue whales, sperm whales, and spinner dolphins are regularly spotted November–April. The crescent beach and surf breaks attract visitors from around the world.

Kandy — Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic

Sri Lanka's royal highland capital and spiritual centre. The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (Sri Dalada Maligawa), a UNESCO World Heritage Site, houses the sacred relic of the Buddha. The annual Kandy Esala Perahera procession is one of Asia's grandest festivals.

Anuradhapura — Ancient Capital

Sri Lanka's first ancient capital, dating back 2,500 years. UNESCO World Heritage ruins include the Ruwanwelisaya dagoba, Jetavanaramaya stupa (once the third-tallest structure on Earth), and the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree.

Polonnaruwa Medieval Kingdom

The medieval capital of Sri Lanka from the 11th–13th centuries. UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring the Gal Vihara rock temple with magnificent carved Buddha figures, the Royal Palace complex, and the Parakrama Samudra reservoir.

Sri Lanka Travel Guide

Visa & Entry

Most nationalities require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) for Sri Lanka. The ETA can be obtained online in advance at eta.gov.lk. Cost: USD 35 for tourist single-entry (30 days, extendable to 90 days).

Best Time to Visit

Sri Lanka has two monsoon seasons. The West & South Coast (Galle, Mirissa, Colombo) and the Cultural Triangle are best visited December–April. The East Coast (Trincomalee, Arugam Bay) is ideal May–September.

Currency

Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). Major credit cards accepted at hotels and larger restaurants. ATMs widely available in Colombo, Kandy, Galle, and major tourist towns.

Getting Around

Scenic train journeys on the Kandy–Ella Hill Country Line (among the world's most beautiful train rides). Tuk-tuks for short hops. Private drivers for cultural triangle circuits. Domestic flights to Jaffna and Batticaloa available.

Sri Lanka Culture & Heritage

Sri Lanka has over 2,500 years of recorded history. With 8 UNESCO World Heritage Sites — more per square kilometre than almost any country — the island is a living museum of Sinhalese, Tamil, Moorish, Portuguese, Dutch, and British heritage.

Traditional arts include Kandyan dance, batik fabric, lacquerwork, ebony woodcarving, and the ancient craft of Dumbara mat weaving. The Sinhala New Year (April 13-14) and the Kandy Esala Perahera (July–August) are must-witness cultural events.
